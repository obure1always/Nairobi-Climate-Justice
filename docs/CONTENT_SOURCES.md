## 1. Statutory, Legal & Municipal Policies (Kenya)

### 1.1 Kenya Climate Change Act 2016 (Act No. 11 of 2016)
- **Publisher:** Republic of Kenya / Kenya Law
- **Official URL:** [https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2016/11/eng%402022-12-31](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2016/11/eng%402022-12-31)
- **Relevance:** Establishes the National Climate Change Council, the Climate Change Directorate, mainstreaming of climate risk into county integrated development plans (CIDPs), and citizen rights to a clean and healthy environment.
- **Classification:** Official Statutory Law.

### 1.2 Nairobi City County Climate Action Plan 2020–2050
- **Publisher:** Nairobi City County Government (NCCG)
- **Official URL:** [https://nairobi.go.ke/climate-action-plan-2020-2050](https://nairobi.go.ke/climate-action-plan-2020-2050)
- **Full Repository Document:** [https://repository.kippra.or.ke/items/8f93f334-1dcc-4bfc-8615-b357180fe067](https://repository.kippra.or.ke/items/8f93f334-1dcc-4bfc-8615-b357180fe067)
- **Relevance:** Outlines municipal emission baselines, vulnerability assessments, urban flooding mitigation, green infrastructure targets, and adaptation priorities across Nairobi's sub-counties (including Kibra and Ruaraka).
- **Classification:** Municipal Government Policy.

### 1.3 Constitution of Kenya (2010) — Articles 10, 42, 43, 69 & 118
- **Publisher:** Kenya Law Reform Commission
- **Official URL:** [https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2010/constitution/eng@2010-09-03](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2010/constitution/eng@2010-09-03)
- **Relevance:** Article 10 establishes public participation as a national value; Article 42 establishes the fundamental right to a clean and healthy environment; Article 43 guarantees basic sanitation and safe water; Article 69 mandates the State to eliminate unsustainable resource exploitation.
- **Classification:** Constitutional Law.

### 1.4 County Public Participation Guidelines (2016)
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Devolution and Planning / Council of Governors
- **Official URL:** [https://knowledgehub.devolution.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/county-public-participation_january-2016.pdf](https://knowledgehub.devolution.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/county-public-participation_january-2016.pdf)
- **Relevance:** Provides the procedural framework for public notice publication, ward-level forums, budget hearings, and memorandum submissions to county assemblies.
- **Classification:** Devolution Governance Manual.

### 1.5 Kenya Policy on Public Participation (Sessional Paper No. 3 of 2023)
- **Publisher:** Office of the Attorney-General and Department of Justice / KLRC
- **Official URL:** [https://www.klrc.go.ke/index.php/reports-and-publications/752-kenya-policy-on-public-participation-sessional-paper-no-3-of-2023](https://www.klrc.go.ke/index.php/reports-and-publications/752-kenya-policy-on-public-participation-sessional-paper-no-3-of-2023)
- **Relevance:** National standard on inclusive civic consultations, youth participation, and institutional accountability.
- **Classification:** Sessional Policy Paper.

## 2. Meteorology, Forecasts & Climate Science

### 2.1 Kenya Meteorological Department (KMD) — Seasonal & Dekadal Forecasts
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Environment, Climate Change and Forestry
- **Official URL:** [https://meteo.go.ke/our-products/seasonal-forecast/](https://meteo.go.ke/our-products/seasonal-forecast/)
- **Live Portal:** [https://meteo.go.ke/](https://meteo.go.ke/)
- **Relevance:** Primary source for official rainfall advisories (Long Rains MAM, Short Rains OND), avoiding reliance on unofficial or misleading social media rumors.
- **Classification:** Official Hydrometeorological Service.
- **Notice to Learners:** Directs users to the live forecast hub for real-time weather rather than citing historical snapshots as current advisories.

### 2.2 Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) — Sixth Assessment Report (AR6)
- **Working Group II:** *Impacts, Adaptation and Vulnerability — Chapter 9: Africa*
- **Publisher:** IPCC / Cambridge University Press
- **Official URL:** [https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/chapter/chapter-9/](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/chapter/chapter-9/)
- **Relevance:** Scientific consensus on African urban warming, precipitation variability, urban heat island dynamics, and climate injustice.
- **Classification:** Global Peer-Reviewed Science.

## 4. Public Health & Disease Prevention

### 4.1 World Health Organization (WHO) — Climate Change and Health
- **Publisher:** World Health Organization
- **Official URL:** [https://www.who.int/teams/environment-climate-change-and-health/climate-change-and-health](https://www.who.int/teams/environment-climate-change-and-health/climate-change-and-health)
- **Relevance:** Establishes the relationship between rising temperatures, compromised water sources, and climate-sensitive diseases.
- **Classification:** Global Public Health Guidance.

### 4.2 WHO Fact Sheet — Heat and Health
- **Publisher:** World Health Organization
- **Official URL:** [https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health)
- **Relevance:** Clinical definitions of heat exhaustion versus heatstroke, vulnerable demographics (elderly, infants, outdoor laborers), and low-cost hydration/ventilation mitigation.
- **Classification:** Public Health Guidance.

### 4.3 Ministry of Health Kenya (MoH) — Guidelines on Cholera and Waterborne Disease Prevention
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Health, Kenya
- **Official URL:** [https://www.health.go.ke/](https://www.health.go.ke/)
- **Relevance:** Safe household water treatment protocols (boiling, certified chlorine solution/WaterGuard usage according to manufacturer label), point-of-use disinfection, and oral rehydration therapy guidance.
- **Classification:** National Public Health Protocol.

## 5. Environmental Claims, Consumer Rights & Greenwashing

### 5.1 United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) — Guidelines for Providing Product Sustainability Information
- **Publisher:** UNEP
- **Official URL:** [https://www.unep.org/resources/report/guidelines-providing-product-sustainability-information](https://www.unep.org/resources/report/guidelines-providing-product-sustainability-information)
- **Relevance:** Principles for credible green claims (reliability, relevance, clarity, transparency, and accessibility). Criteria to detect greenwashing.
- **Classification:** International Environmental Guidance.

### 5.2 Competition Authority of Kenya (CAK) — Consumer Protection Guidelines
- **Publisher:** Competition Authority of Kenya
- **Official URL:** [https://cak.go.ke/consumer-protection](https://cak.go.ke/consumer-protection)
- **Relevance:** Section 55 & 56 of the Competition Act, prohibiting false or misleading representations regarding the nature, characteristics, or suitability of goods.
- **Classification:** Statutory Consumer Protection.

## 6. Climate Justice, Adaptation Education & Youth Engagement

### 6.1 UNFCCC — Opportunities and Options for Enhancing Adaptation Action Through Education and Training
- **Publisher:** United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change
- **Official URL:** [https://unfccc.int/topics/adaptation-and-resilience/resources/publications/opportunities-and-options-for-enhancing-adaptation-action-through-education-and-training-and-public](https://unfccc.int/topics/adaptation-and-resilience/resources/publications/opportunities-and-options-for-enhancing-adaptation-action-through-education-and-training-and-public)
- **Relevance:** Principles of Action for Climate Empowerment (ACE), community-led adaptation, and participatory youth education.
- **Classification:** Multilateral Climate Education Report.

### 6.2 Stockholm Environment Institute (SEI) Africa — Informal Settlements and Climate Resilience in Nairobi
- **Publisher:** SEI Africa Centre, Nairobi
- **Official URL:** [https://www.sei.org/centres/africa/](https://www.sei.org/centres/africa/)
- **Relevance:** Research documenting localized microclimates, drainage bottlenecks, and socioeconomic vulnerability in Kibera and Mukuru.
- **Classification:** Peer-Reviewed Policy Research.
